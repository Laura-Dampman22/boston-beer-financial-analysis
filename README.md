# Boston Beer Financial Statement Analysis

Financial statement analysis of The Boston Beer Company using Microsoft Excel, comparative analysis, common-size statements, financial ratios, and financial performance evaluation.

## Project Overview

This project was completed for my Financial Analysis course and focuses on evaluating the financial performance of The Boston Beer Company.

Using financial data from 2021 through 2023, I analyzed the company's financial statements to identify trends in its financial performance, liquidity, profitability, capital structure, cash flow, and overall financial position.

The project goes beyond calculating financial figures and ratios by interpreting what the results reveal about the company's performance. The analysis identifies areas where Boston Beer performed well, as well as areas that may require improvement to support sustainable financial growth.

## Project Goals

- Analyze Boston Beer's financial performance over a three-year period
- Compare financial statement data from 2021 through 2023
- Identify significant changes and trends in financial performance
- Perform common-size and comparative financial statement analysis
- Calculate and interpret financial ratios
- Evaluate liquidity, profitability, debt, and investor-related measures
- Analyze the company's sources of funding and capital structure
- Identify areas of financial strength
- Identify areas where financial performance could be improved
- Use financial data to develop business conclusions and recommendations

## Analysis Performed

The project includes several forms of financial analysis:

### Comparative Financial Statement Analysis

Financial statement values were compared across 2021, 2022, and 2023 to evaluate changes in the company's financial position and performance over time.

### Common-Size Analysis

Common-size financial statements were used to evaluate individual financial statement accounts relative to key totals and identify changes in the composition of the company's financial position.

### Financial Ratio Analysis

Financial ratios and additional calculations were used to evaluate different areas of company performance, including:

- Liquidity
- Profitability
- Debt and financial leverage
- Earnings per share
- Investor-related performance
- Asset utilization
- Capital structure
- Cash flow and funding

### Financial Performance Interpretation

The calculated results were analyzed to determine the story behind the financial data.

Areas of strength identified in the analysis included cash generation, cost management, debt management, maintaining a stable share count, and increasing earnings per share.

The analysis also examined areas of concern, including declining sales, increasing accounts receivable, the use of cash and short-term investments, investor sentiment, and the importance of balancing short-term financial stability with long-term growth.
